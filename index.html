BEHIND THE WORDPLAY

About Me

My name is Gülefşan Soslu. I am a Computer Engineering student at Çankaya University. I have wanted to become a computer engineer since I was a child. Coding is one of my main interests.

I also have an associate degree in Computer Programming from Eskişehir Technical University. I used the knowledge from this programme while developing this website.

I started the English Preparatory Programme on 28 September 2026. I am currently studying at A2 level. I enjoy this learning experience and learn many new things in my classes.

About Gül’s Wordplay

Gül’s Wordplay is my project for the A2 Co-Curricular Activity Assignment. When I first read the assignment, I immediately wanted to create a website. This project brings together my interest in coding and my English studies.

Vocabulary is one of the most difficult parts of learning English for me. For this reason, I wanted to create a website for people who have similar difficulties. All the words on this website come from my weekly Vocabulary Notebook assignments.

The website includes word definitions, Turkish meanings, example sentences, memes and practice questions. My aim is to make vocabulary practice useful and enjoyable through everyday humour.

I used ChatGPT and Gemini for support during the development process. I also used my previous programming knowledge. In the future, I hope to build larger websites. This small project is an important step towards that goal.
